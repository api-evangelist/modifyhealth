Low FODMAP Diet Beginner's Guide Dealing with digestive issues can be frustrating and debilitating. For those suffering from conditions like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), the Low FODMAP diet has emerged as a promising solution. FODMAPs (Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides, and Polyols) are a group of carbohydrates that can trigger digestive symptoms in susceptible individuals.
