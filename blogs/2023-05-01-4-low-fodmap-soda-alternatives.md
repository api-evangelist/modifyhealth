5 Low FODMAP Soda Alternatives Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) can be a challenging condition to manage, especially when it comes to finding suitable drinks. Sodas sweetened with high fructose corn syrup, for instance, are high in FODMAPs and can trigger symptoms such as bloating, abdominal pain, and diarrhea. Luckily, there are many low FODMAP beverages that people with IBS can enjoy instead of sodas.
