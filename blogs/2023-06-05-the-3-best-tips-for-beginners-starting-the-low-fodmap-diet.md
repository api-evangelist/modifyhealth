The 3 Best Tips For Beginners Starting the Low FODMAP Diet Embarking on a new dietary journey can be both exciting and challenging. The Low FODMAP diet is gaining popularity as an effective approach for managing digestive issues such as irritable bowel syndrome (IBS). FODMAPs are a group of carbohydrates that can trigger symptoms in sensitive individuals.
