Completing The Elimination Phase The Low FODMAP diet has gained significant recognition for its effectiveness in managing irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) symptoms and other gastrointestinal disorders. The elimination phase of this diet protocol plays a crucial role in identifying trigger foods that can cause digestive discomfort. In this blog post, we will guide you through the steps to successfully complete the elimination phase of the Low FODMAP diet, helping you regain control of your digestive health.
