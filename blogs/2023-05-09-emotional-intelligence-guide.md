Emotional Intelligence Guide Emotional intelligence (EI) is a critical skill that plays a significant role in our personal and professional lives. It is the ability to recognize, understand, and manage our own emotions while also being able to perceive and respond to the emotions of others. In recent years, the importance of EI has gained widespread recognition as it has been shown to be a significant predictor of success in both personal and professional spheres.
